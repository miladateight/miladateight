I am an IT Lead in an EPC company, and the job goes far past day-to-day support. I design, run and troubleshoot business infrastructure: networks, servers, secure access, web and mail services, backups, monitoring and technical continuity.

I work across every layer — from physical cabling and packet flow up to operating systems, services, domains, applications and the person actually using them. Alongside that I work on web design, corporate and technical presentations, documentation and visual communication, turning complex technical material into something a manager or a user can act on.

I do not force one favourite tool onto every problem. I look at the architecture, the constraints, the risk and the expected outcome first, then pick a solution that can be tested, documented and maintained.
